Rules & Violations

🟡 Light Violations - Teguran

1. Late arrival to competition arena without valid reason
2. Improper uniform, missing belt or safety equipment
3. Talking, shouting or making noise during match
4. Leaving competition area without referee permission
5. Delaying match by adjusting uniform repeatedly

🟠 Moderate Violations - Peringatan

1. Intentional pushing, shoving or pulling opponent
2. Grabbing opponent's uniform, hair or body parts
3. Unsportsmanlike conduct, gestures or language
4. Arguing with referee or challenging judges decision
5. Continuing attack after referee's stop command

🔴 Serious Violations - Hukuman

1. Striking prohibited areas: groin, throat, back of head, spine
2. Intentional attempt to cause serious injury to opponent
3. Disrespecting referee, judges or match officials
4. Using illegal techniques: eye gouging, biting, joint locks
5. Throwing opponent on head or neck dangerously

⚫ Disqualification - Diskualifikasi

1. Repeated serious violations after 2 warnings
2. Physical assault on referee, judge or officials
3. Use of performance enhancing drugs or substances
4. Match fixing, bribery or deliberate cheating
5. Causing opponent unable to continue due to illegal attack

⚖️ Penalty - Sanksi

1. Teguran: Verbal warning from referee, no point deduction
2. Peringatan 1: First warning, -1 point deduction
3. Peringatan 2: Second warning, -2 points deduction
4. Peringatan 3: Third warning leads to Disqualification
5. Ban: Prohibited from tournaments for specific period
