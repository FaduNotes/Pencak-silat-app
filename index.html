What is Pencak Silat?

Pencak Silat is a traditional Indonesian martial art. It combines striking, grappling, and weaponry. In sport format, it has 2 main categories: Tanding and Seni.

Tanding - Match Rules

  • Duration: 3 rounds × 2 minutes, 1 min rest
  • Scoring Area: 10m × 10m mat
  • Points: Punch = 1, Kick = 2, Throw = 3
  • Win by: Highest points, KO, or DQ
